Welcome to Claude Chrome under WSL

Claude Code runs inside WSL, and now it can open, read and click through web pages in this browser. This page is the proof: setup opened it here.

View the project on GitHub tirandagan.com
  1. 1
    Just askTell Claude “open the pricing page on example.com and tell me what it says.”
  2. 2
    It works in its own tabClaude opens a tab of its own and closes it when done. Your tabs stay yours.
  3. 3
    You stay in charge of sign-insClaude never types your passwords. When a page needs a login, you sign in and Claude carries on.
